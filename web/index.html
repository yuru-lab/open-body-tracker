open-body-tracker

Single-camera full-body 3D pose in your browser (WebGPU). Video never leaves your device.

Press Start. The first run downloads the model (190–380 MB, cached afterwards).

Blue = left, orange = right. Box: yellow = found by the detector, green dashed = following the body. Drag the 3D view to rotate.

Code and model weights: Apache-2.0 · trained in part on labels from SAM 3D Body (Meta) · person detector: YOLOX (Apache-2.0). Research software; not a certified measuring device.
